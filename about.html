About Us

Who We Are

Our Story

Founded by community, for community

In 2025, immigrants, refugees, and U.S. citizens in North Carolina came together to establish RILOC as a consortium focused not only on serving communities, but on strengthening the organizations already rooted within those communities.

RILOC operates from a consortium perspective: connect, strengthen, empower, and sustain. Our long-term goal is to help create an ecosystem in which refugee and immigrant-led organizations can access the knowledge, resources, partnerships, and opportunities necessary to become stronger and more sustainable.

Mission

Empowering community-led change

To empower refugee and immigrant-led organizations and the communities they serve through capacity building, leadership development, economic empowerment, advocacy, and strategic partnerships.

Vision

Inclusive, resilient communities

To create inclusive, resilient, and empowered communities where refugee and immigrant-led organizations have the capacity, resources, and leadership necessary to shape their own futures and contribute meaningfully to society.

Core Values

The principles behind every decision

Inclusion

Everyone deserves to participate fully in the social, economic, civic, and community life of society.

Empowerment

We enable individuals and organizations to develop their own capacity and exercise agency.

Equity

We identify and address barriers that prevent communities from accessing opportunities and resources.

Integrity

We promote honesty, transparency, accountability, and responsible stewardship of resources.

Collaboration

Lasting change is strengthened through partnerships, shared knowledge, and collective action.

Respect for Lived Experience

We recognize refugees and immigrants as leaders, contributors, advocates, and decision-makers.

Sustainability

We seek solutions that strengthen organizations and communities for the long term.

Who We Serve

Organizations and communities at the center

●Refugee-led and immigrant-led organizations
●Community-based and emerging grassroots organizations
●Refugee and immigrant leaders and advocates
●Youth, young adults, and underserved families
●The broader communities in which refugee and immigrant populations live, work, and thrive
The Coalition Model

Stronger together

RILOC functions as a network and coalition, creating opportunities for organizations to learn from one another rather than working in isolation.

● Shared knowledge & technical assistance
● Capacity-building opportunities
● Leadership development
● Collaborative fundraising
● Resource sharing & peer learning
● Joint initiatives & strategic partnerships
● Increased organizational visibility
● Collective advocacy
Governance

Accountable and transparent

RILOC is committed to developing strong governance systems that promote accountability, transparency, ethical leadership, responsible financial management, community representation, and strategic decision-making.

Geographic Scope

North Carolina roots, national vision

Founded in North Carolina, RILOC begins its work from the state's diverse refugee and immigrant communities. Our long-term vision is to develop a broader network connecting organizations across the United States.
