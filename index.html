Who We Are

Strengthening organizations that know their communities best

RILOC was established on the belief that individuals and organizations with lived experience of migration are uniquely positioned to understand the challenges, aspirations, and opportunities within refugee and immigrant communities.

Rather than speaking for communities, we strengthen their capacity to lead, organize, advocate, and create sustainable solutions.
